    General payment terms for ZIABRIDGE services.

    The following outlines the general payment terms for services provided by ZIABRIDGE. Specific terms may be discussed and confirmed individually for each project.

    Service Agreement

    Payment terms will be confirmed in writing (email or agreement) before a service begins, based on the scope of inspection, sourcing, or development work requested.

    Certain services may require a partial advance payment to confirm booking, with the remaining balance due upon completion or delivery of the report.

    Payment methods will be shared directly with the client during service confirmation, and may include bank transfer or other mutually agreed methods for international clients.

    Refund eligibility depends on the stage of service completion and will be assessed on a case-by-case basis.
