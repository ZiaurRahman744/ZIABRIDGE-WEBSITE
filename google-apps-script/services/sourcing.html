    Sourcing Solutions

    ZIABRIDGE provides sourcing support to help businesses find suitable production partners and manage apparel supply solutions.

    Our goal is to create reliable connections between global buyers and capable manufacturers.

    Bangladesh is the world's second-largest garment exporter, with thousands of factories and buying houses operating across the country — but only a fraction of them are formally registered with BGMEA, and quality, capability and reliability vary widely between them. For a buyer sourcing from outside the country, the gap between finding a genuinely reliable partner and an unreliable one can be difficult to see from a website or a sample alone. That's the gap ZIABRIDGE is built to close.

    How We Vet Sourcing Partners

    • Registration & Membership Check — confirming BGMEA or relevant association membership and verifying the factory's registration status before recommending them.
    • Compliance Verification — checking for recognized certifications such as ISO 9001, BSCI, or WRAP where relevant to your product category and market requirements.
    • Capability Matching — matching your product type, order volume and price target against factories that genuinely specialize in that category, rather than a generic list.
    • Reference & Track Record Review — looking at export experience and buyer history rather than relying on a factory's own marketing claims.

    Once a suitable partner is identified, we support costing, sample coordination and production communication, so sourcing a new factory feels less like a leap of faith and more like a managed, transparent process.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I know if a Bangladesh garment factory is reliable? +
    Check for BGMEA (or relevant association) registration, ask for recognized compliance certifications such as ISO 9001 or BSCI, and request references from recent export orders. A reliable partner will be transparent about all of this — we verify these details as part of every sourcing recommendation.
    What's the difference between a sourcing agent and a buying house? +
    A sourcing agent mainly helps find factories and negotiate terms, while a buying house typically offers broader support including merchandising, sampling, costing and order follow-up. In practice many providers, including ZIABRIDGE, offer both, so it's worth confirming exactly what's included before you commit.
    Can you help source from a factory I've already identified? +
    Yes. We can independently verify a factory you've already found — checking registration, compliance and capability — before you commit to an order, which is especially useful if you're working with a supplier for the first time.
    What information do you need to start a sourcing search? +
    Product category, target price range, order volume, and any specific compliance or certification requirements. The more detail you can share upfront, the faster we can match you with genuinely suitable factories rather than a generic list.
