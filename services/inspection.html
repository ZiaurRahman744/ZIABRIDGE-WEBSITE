    Inspection & Quality Services

    ZIABRIDGE provides professional inspection and quality assurance solutions to help brands, buyers and businesses maintain international quality standards.

    Our inspection services support every stage of production by identifying quality issues, improving compliance and ensuring customer confidence.

    Working with garment manufacturers across Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj and Chattogram, we help international buyers avoid the most common risks in Bangladesh sourcing: shipments that look fine at a glance but fail once they reach the customer. A single missed defect discovered after goods have already shipped is far more costly than one caught during production — in returns, chargebacks, and damaged buyer relationships. That's why we treat inspection as a staged process rather than a single end-of-line check.

    How Our Inspection Process Works

    A reliable quality control process for apparel typically runs across three checkpoints, and our approach follows the same staged structure used across the industry:

    • Fabric & Cutting Inspection — before bulk sewing begins, fabric is checked against the approved specification for GSM, shade consistency and flaws, and cut panels are verified against the pattern.
    • In-Line Inspection — conducted while the order is roughly 30–40% complete, catching stitching, construction and workmanship issues early enough to correct before the full run is affected.
    • Final Random Inspection (Pre-Shipment) — performed once 80–100% of the order is finished and export-packed, using AQL-based random sampling to verify the shipment against your approved specification before it leaves the factory.

    Every inspection checks measurements, stitching quality, fabric defects, trims and accessories, labeling and packaging, and colorfastness where applicable, with a detailed photo-documented report delivered before you approve shipment.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is AQL and why does it matter for my order? +
    AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) is the international sampling standard (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 & ISO 2859-1) used to decide how many units from a shipment to inspect and how many defects are acceptable before a lot is rejected. Instead of checking every single piece, a statistically valid random sample gives a reliable picture of the whole shipment's quality, saving time while still protecting you from receiving a defective batch.
    When should a pre-shipment inspection be scheduled? +
    A pre-shipment inspection should be scheduled once your order is at least 80% complete and export-packed, so the inspector can evaluate a shipment that accurately represents what will actually be loaded. Booking too early, before enough units are finished, gives an incomplete picture of the final shipment.
    What kind of defects are most commonly found during garment inspection in Bangladesh? +
    Based on inspection experience across Bangladesh's garment sector, the most frequent issues are cosmetic imperfections, measurement deviations, incorrect or missing labeling, packaging damage, functional failures (like zippers or closures), color inconsistencies and missing accessories. Setting a clear, written inspection checklist before production begins helps prevent most of these from reaching the final shipment.
    Can inspection help avoid disputes with my factory? +
    Yes. Agreeing on remediation terms in writing before production starts — rework, replacement or a price adjustment — gives both sides a clear, pre-agreed path if a batch does not pass inspection. A documented, photo-based report from an independent inspection also gives you objective evidence to reference if a disagreement comes up later.
    How soon after inspection do I receive the report? +
    You receive a full inspection report, including defect photos and AQL sampling results, so you can make a clear ship or hold decision without delay.
