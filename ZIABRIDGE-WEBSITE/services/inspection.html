Inspection Standard (AQL Reference)

ZIABRIDGE inspections follow international AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) standards based on ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 & ISO 2859-1, General Inspection Level II. Buyers may choose their preferred AQL level.
