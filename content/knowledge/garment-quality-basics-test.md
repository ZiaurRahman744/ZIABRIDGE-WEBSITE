## What Is Garment Quality?

Garment quality simply means how closely a finished piece of clothing matches what the buyer approved — in fabric, colour, measurement, workmanship, and finish. It is not a fixed, universal standard; it is whatever was agreed in the specification and the approved sample for that specific order.

This is why the very first thing every quality check compares against is not an abstract idea of "good clothing" — it is the buyer's own paperwork and sample.

## Two Reference Points Every Inspection Uses

- **Buyer Specification (Spec Sheet):** A written document listing exact requirements.
- **Approved Sample:** A physical garment, signed off by the buyer.
